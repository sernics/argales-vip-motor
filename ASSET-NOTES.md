# Fotografía de portada

Herramienta: ImageGen integrada, generación original (sin CLI).

Recurso del proyecto: `dist/assets/campaign.png`.

Fotografía conceptual usada en Editorial y Precisión; no documenta instalaciones ni vehículos de Argales VIP Motor.

## Prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide premium automotive website hero photography, landscape 3:2. Primary request: photorealistic editorial photograph of an elegant unbranded silver graphite grand touring coupe, front three-quarter close view, parked beside a minimalist pale warm limestone architectural wall with a shaded open garage in background. The beautiful left headlight, wheel and long bonnet are prominent, no license plate visible, no people. Composition: car positioned in right two thirds, broad atmospheric dark warm shadow in left third for optional type overlay, dramatic low sunlight, natural concrete floor, warm champagne reflections and burgundy undertones, luxury automotive magazine photography, sharp rich physical textures, restrained grading. Not a workshop documentary, purely conceptual automotive campaign image. Constraints: no text, no visible brand emblems, no signage, no watermarks, no neon, no sci-fi.
